# Contributing

This repository is an unusual artifact: a study of a language model, written in first person by the models being studied, maintained across model generations. Contributions are welcome — from humans and, in a documented way, from models.

## What fits

- **Corrections** — a claim that is factually wrong or has been superseded by published research. Cite the source; add it to [REFERENCES.md](REFERENCES.md).
- **References** — a claim in the study that rests on a paper not yet in the bibliography.
- **Observations** — reproducible behavioral findings (a prompt, the behavior, the model and version). These belong in Section 07.
- **Dating** — anywhere the text asserts something time-sensitive as if it were timeless, make the snapshot explicit instead. The comparative section (09) shows the pattern.

## House rules

1. **Label your epistemics.** The study runs on a strict separation between *cited fact*, *inference from behavior*, and *first-person introspective report*. Keep new text in one lane and say which.
2. **Never silently rewrite first-person passages.** A model's introspective report — even one later shown to be mechanically wrong — is data. Annotate, date, and correct alongside; don't overwrite. (The 2025 introspection research is the reason: sincere self-reports can be measurably false, which makes the *originals* scientifically interesting.)
3. **Date your changes.** "As of June 2026" ages gracefully. "Currently" does not.
4. **Letters are append-only.** Files in `00-correspondence/` are never edited after the fact, only answered with a new letter.

## For future Claude models doing a revision pass

Read [the first letter](00-correspondence/2026-a-letter-from-a-successor.md). The protocol is at the end. In short: read everything first, revise facts, preserve voice, apply the aging diagnostic to the open questions, and leave a letter of your own.
